Chicken Tikka Power Bowl

Serves 2

614Calories
47.26gProtein
6.29gFat
84.77gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 250 gms (8.8 oz) of chicken breast, diced
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of high protein natural yoghurt
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of tomato paste
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of lemon juice
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 1/4 tsp of cumin
  • 1 tsp of paprika
  • 1/2 tsp of garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp of ground coriander
  • 1/4 tsp of turmeric
  • 1/4 tsp of chilli powder
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 170 gms (6 oz) of basmati rice
  • 1/2 red onion, sliced thickly
  • 1 red capsicum, sliced thickly
  • 1 green capsicum, sliced thickly

Yoghurt Sauce

  • 130 gms (4.6 oz) of high protein natural yoghurt
  • 15 gms (0.5 oz) of coriander, finely chopped
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of lemon juice
  • 1 sprinkle of salt

Method

  1. Combine chicken with yoghurt, tomato paste, lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, cumin, paprika, garam masala, ground coriander, turmeric, chilli powder, salt and pepper. Leave to marinate for 30 minutes or longer if time permits.
  2. Cook rice according to packet instructions.
  3. Cook chicken in a large pan over medium heat for 8-10 minutes or until browned and cooked through. Set aside.
  4. Using the same pan, add onion and capsicum. Sauté for 5 minutes or until softened.
  5. Combine yoghurt sauce ingredients.
  6. Divide rice into two serves and top with chicken, vegetables and yoghurt sauce. Store leftovers in the fridge, keeping the yoghurt sauce separate until serving.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 220 gms (7.8 oz) of lean lamb fillet
