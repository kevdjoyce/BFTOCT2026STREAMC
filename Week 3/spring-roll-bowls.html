Spring Roll Bowls

Serves 4

604Calories
30.29gProtein
26.24gFat
58.86gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 250 gms (8.8 oz) of vermicelli noodles
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of ginger, grated or finely diced
  • 500 gms (17.6 oz) of lean pork mince
  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of mushrooms, finely chopped
  • 400 gms (14.1 oz) of coleslaw cabbage mix
  • 3/4 spring onion bunch, sliced
  • 125 mls (4.2 fl oz) of chicken stock
  • 30 mls (1 fl oz) of soy sauce
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of oyster sauce
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of sesame oil
  • 40 gms (1.4 oz) of sesame seeds

Method

  1. Cook noodles according to packet instructions.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add garlic and ginger and cook, stirring for 1 minute.
  3. Add the mushrooms and cook, stirring, for 3 minutes or until mushrooms have softened.
  4. Add the pork mince and break apart. Cook for 5-8 minutes or until browned.
  5. Add the coleslaw cabbage mix and spring onion. Stir and heat for 5 minutes.
  6. Meanwhile, prepare the sauce. Combine stock, soy sauce, oyster sauce and sesame oil. Add to pan and toss to combine.
  7. Divide noodles into four serves and top with pork mixture. Store leftovers in the fridge. When ready to serve, sprinkle with sesame seeds.

Notes

*GF adaptation: ensure noodles are gluten free and use a gluten free soy sauce or tamari

**Suggested protein substitution: 500 gms (17.6 oz) of chicken mince
