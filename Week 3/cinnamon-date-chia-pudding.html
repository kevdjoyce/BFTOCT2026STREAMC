Cinnamon Date Chia Pudding

Serves 4

600Calories
45.36gProtein
23.63gFat
46.46gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 800 gms (28.2 oz) of high protein natural yoghurt
  • 80 gms (2.8 oz) of chia seeds
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of dates, pitted and finely chopped
  • 2 tsp of cinnamon
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of honey
  • 800 mls (27.1 fl oz) of reduced fat milk
  • 60 gms (2.1 oz) of whey protein powder
  • 80 gms (2.8 oz) of walnuts, roughly chopped

Method

  1. Add all ingredients except walnuts to a bowl and mix thoroughly until combined. Add a dash of water if needed to thin.
  2. Cover and leave in the fridge overnight until chia seeds have absorbed the liquid.
  3. Divide into four serves and top with walnuts.
