Protein Carrot Cake Bites

Serves 12

165Calories
6.23gProtein
10.83gFat
9.64gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 120 gms (4.2 oz) of dates, pitted
  • 180 gms (6.3 oz) of walnuts
  • 1 1/2 tsp of vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp of cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp of ground ginger
  • 3/4 tsp of nutmeg
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of whey protein powder
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of rolled oats

Method

  1. Add all ingredients into a food processor and blend until a loose dough forms. Add a small amount of water if mixture is too dry.
  2. Roll dough into 12 balls.
  3. Store in an airtight container in the fridge or freezer.
