Tuna & Sundried Tomato Rice Cakes

Serves 1

597Calories
36.08gProtein
28.69gFat
42.95gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 1 x 95g tin (3.4 oz) of tuna in olive oil, drained
  • 1/3 lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of reduced fat tasty cheese
  • 35 gms (1.2 oz) of sundried tomatoes, drained and finely chopped
  • 6 multigrain rice cakes
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of baby spinach
  • 1/2 avocado

Method

  1. Combine tuna and sundried tomatoes.
  2. Top rice cakes with avocado, cheese, spinach and tuna and sundried tomato mix, tomatoes and cucumber.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 150 gms (5.3 oz) of chicken breast
