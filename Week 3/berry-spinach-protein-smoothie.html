Berry Spinach Protein Smoothie

Serves 1

608Calories
44.23gProtein
31.93gFat
28.36gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 150 gms (5.3 oz) of frozen mixed berries
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of baby spinach
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of whey protein powder
  • 300 mls (10.1 fl oz) of reduced fat milk
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of chia seeds
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of walnuts

Method

  1. Blitz all ingredients together in a high speed blender until smooth.
