Tomato, Basil & Fetta Omelette

Serves 1

590 kcalCalories
37.7 gProtein
38.72 gFat
19.67 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 75 gms (2.6 oz) of cherry tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 1/4 red onion, diced finely
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of tomato paste
  • 4 eggs, whisked
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of fetta, crumbled
  • 1/2 x 15g (0.5 oz) punnet of basil, stems removed
  • 1 slice of wholegrain bread, toasted

Method

  1. Heat 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add tomato, garlic, onion and salt, stirring for 7-8 minutes or until tomato begins to break down.
  2. Add tomato paste and cook for another minute. Transfer to a bowl.
  3. Turn heat down to medium-low and heat the remaining oil. Add the eggs and cook until nearly set. Add the tomato mixture, fetta and basil and gently fold the omelette in half. Remove from heat and serve with a slice of toast.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free bread
