Simple Dhal & Rice

Serves 2

624 kcalCalories
32.55 gProtein
8.81 gFat
92.86 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 190 gms (6.7 oz) of dried red split lentils, rinsed
  • 750 mls (25.4 fl oz) of water
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of frozen peas
  • 1 carrot, finely diced
  • 3/4 cauliflower, cut into small florets
  • 1 tsp of turmeric
  • 1 tsp of chicken stock powder
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of margarine
  • 80 gms (2.8 oz) of basmati rice

Method

  1. Add lentils, water, peas, carrot, cauliflower, turmeric and stock powder to a pot and cook on medium-high heat for 20 minutes or until liquid has been absorbed and lentils have become mushy. Stir through the margarine.
  2. While lentils are cooking, cook rice according to packet instructions.
  3. Divide rice into two serves and top with dhal. Store leftovers in the fridge.
