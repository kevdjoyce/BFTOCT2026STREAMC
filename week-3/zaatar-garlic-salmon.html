Zaatar Garlic Salmon

Serves 1

609 kcalCalories
37.72 gProtein
41.42 gFat
19.85 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of potatoes, cubed
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of cherry tomatoes
  • 1/4 cauliflower, cut into smaller florets
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 sprinkles of salt
  • 2 sprinkles of pepper
  • 1 tsp of ground coriander
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 2 tsp of zaatar spice
  • 150 gms (5.3 oz) of salmon fillet

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200 degrees Celsius (400 F) and prepare an ovenproof dish.
  2. Add potatoes, tomatoes and cauliflower to a large bowl and combine with 15 mls (0.5 fl oz) of olive oil, salt, pepper, coriander, half the garlic and half the zaatar spice. Transfer to baking dish in an even layer.
  3. Bake in the oven for 25 minutes. Remove from oven.
  4. Season salmon with salt, pepper and remaining zaatar. Transfer to baking dish and bake in the oven for another 15-20 minutes.
  5. Serve salmon with vegetables.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 300 gms (10.6 oz) of white fish fillet
