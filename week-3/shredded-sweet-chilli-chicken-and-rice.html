Shredded Sweet Chilli Chicken & Rice

Serves 2

602 kcalCalories
43.55 gProtein
12.8 gFat
74.08 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 160 gms (5.6 oz) of basmati rice
  • 300 gms (10.6 oz) of chicken breast
  • 1 tsp of garlic powder
  • 1 tsp of paprika
  • 30 mls (1 fl oz) of soy sauce
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 60 mls (2 fl oz) of light sweet chilli sauce
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of white wine vinegar
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of sesame oil
  • 1/4 spring onion bunch, sliced
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of frozen peas
  • 1 carrot, diced

Method

  1. Cook rice according to packet instructions. Allow to cool.
  2. Season chicken with garlic powder, paprika and 5 mls of soy sauce.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium-high heat and cook chicken for 8-10 minutes each side or until cooked through.
  4. Remove chicken from pan and then shred.
  5. Mix together the sweet chilli sauce, 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of soy sauce and white wine vinegar to make the sauce. Pour into the pan and as it starts to bubble, add the shredded chicken and coat in the sauce.
  6. In another large pan, heat sesame oil over medium-high heat. Add spring onion, peas and carrot and cook for 5 minutes. Mix in the rice and remaining soy sauce. Heat for another 3-5 minutes or until vegetables have softened.
  7. Divide rice into two serves and top with chicken.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free soy sauce or tamari

**Suggested protein substitution: 300 gms (10.6 oz) of lean pork fillet
