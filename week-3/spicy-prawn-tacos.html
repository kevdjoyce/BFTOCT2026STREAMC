Spicy Prawn Tacos

Serves 2

626 kcalCalories
42.8 gProtein
20.34 gFat
64.19 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 250 gms (8.8 oz) of prawns
  • 2 sprinkles of salt
  • 2 sprinkles of pepper
  • 1/2 tsp of garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp of paprika
  • 1/4 tsp of onion powder
  • 15 mls (0.5 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 70 gms (2.5 oz) of reduced fat mayonnaise
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of sriracha
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of water
  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of coleslaw cabbage mix
  • 15 gms (0.5 oz) of coriander, roughly chopped
  • 40 gms (1.4 oz) of reduced fat tasty cheese, shredded
  • 8 mini tortillas (~25 gms ((0.9 oz)) each)
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of baby spinach
  • 2/3 lebanese cucumber, sliced

Method

  1. Pat the prawns dry with a paper towel and place in a bowl. Season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika, onion powder and olive oil.
  2. Heat a pan over medium-high heat and cook the prawns for 1-2 minutes each side or until cooked.
  3. Combine mayonnaise, sriracha and water. Toss with coleslaw cabbage mix, coriander and cheese. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. When ready to serve, toast tortillas in a pan and layer with spinach, cucumber, coleslaw and prawns. Store leftovers in the fridge separately.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free corn tortillas

**Suggested protein substitution: 230 gms (8.1 oz) of white fish fillet
