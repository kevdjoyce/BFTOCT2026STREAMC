Mongolian Beef Fried Rice

Serves 2

608Calories
41.57gProtein
14.21gFat
71.72gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 130 gms (4.6 oz) of basmati rice
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of brown sugar
  • 40 mls (1.4 fl oz) of hoisin sauce
  • 30 mls (1 fl oz) of soy sauce
  • 30 mls (1 fl oz) of water
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 250 gms (8.8 oz) of lean beef mince
  • 2 eggs, whisked
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of ginger, grated or finely chopped
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 1 broccoli, finely chopped
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 75 gms (2.6 oz) of frozen peas
  • 1/4 spring onion bunch, sliced

Method

  1. Cook rice according to packet instructions.
  2. Whisk together the brown sugar, hoisin sauce, soy sauce and water. Set aside.
  3. Heat oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Cook beef mince for 5-7 minutes.
  4. Push beef to one side and scramble the eggs.
  5. Then, add ginger and garlic and cook, stirring, for 1 minute.
  6. Add broccoli, carrot and peas and stir fry for 2-3 minutes.
  7. Add in the cooked rice and spring onion and sauce. Combine and heat for 5 minutes or until heated through.
  8. Divide into two serves and store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free soy sauce or tamari

**Suggested protein substitution: 250 gms (8.8 oz) of lean lamb mince
