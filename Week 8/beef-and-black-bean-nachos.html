Beef & Black Bean Nachos

Serves 2

618Calories
50.51gProtein
19.95gFat
52.38gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 2 wholegrain wraps
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/4 red onion, sliced
  • 250 gms (8.8 oz) of lean beef mince
  • 1/2 x 400g (14.1 oz) tin of black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1/2 red capsicum, diced
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of taco seasoning
  • 60 gms (2.1 oz) of reduced fat tasty cheese, grated
  • 60 gms (2.1 oz) of salsa
  • 60 gms (2.1 oz) of high protein natural yoghurt
  • 1/4 spring onion bunch, sliced

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200 degrees Celsius (400 F) and line a tray with baking paper.
  2. Cut wrap into pieces and place on baking tray. Heat in the oven for 5-10 minutes or until crispy.
  3. Meanwhile, heat olive oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Add onion and cook for 3 minutes.
  4. Add mince and break apart. Cook for 6 minutes.
  5. Add black beans, capsicum and taco seasoning. Cook for another 5-8 minutes.
  6. Layer tortilla chips with mince mixture and top with cheese. Return to oven for another 5-10 minutes or until cheese has melted.
  7. Divide into two serves. Dollop with salsa and yoghurt and sprinkle with spring onion.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free pita bread or wrap

**Suggested protein substitution: 250 gms (8.8 oz) of lean lamb mince
