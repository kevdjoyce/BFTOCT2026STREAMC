Almond Croissant Baked Oats

Yield: 4 servings
Cook time: 40 minutes

Per serve: 605 kcal, protein 45.74 g, fat 20.6 g, carbs 56.35 g

Ingredients

  • 2 bananas, mashed
  • 40 mls (1.4 fl oz) of maple syrup
  • 375 mls (12.7 fl oz) of reduced fat milk
  • 150 gms (5.3 oz) of rolled oats
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of almond flour
  • 60 gms (2.1 oz) of whey protein powder
  • 1 tsp of baking powder
  • 80 gms (2.8 oz) of flaked almonds
  • 800 gms (28.2 oz) of high protein natural yoghurt

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180 degrees Celsius (350 F).
  2. In an ovenproof dish, mix together the banana, maple syrup, milk, oats, almond flour, protein powder and baking powder.
  3. Level mixture and top with flaked almonds.
  4. Bake in the oven for 40 minutes or until golden and set.
  5. Allow to cool before slicing into four serves.
  6. Store covered in the fridge. When ready to serve, warm and top with 200 gms (7.1 oz) of yoghurt.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use quinoa flakes or brown rice flakes instead of rolled oats.
