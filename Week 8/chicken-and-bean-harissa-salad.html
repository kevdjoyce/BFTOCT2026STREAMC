Chicken & Bean Harissa Salad

Serves 4

611Calories
57.82gProtein
22.12gFat
33.47gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 600 gms (21.2 oz) of chicken breast
  • 15 mls (0.5 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tsp of paprika
  • 2 tsp of garlic powder
  • 1 tsp of oregano
  • 2 sprinkles of salt
  • 2 sprinkles of pepper
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of high protein natural yoghurt
  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of fetta, crumbled
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of lemon juice
  • 40 gms (1.4 oz) of harissa paste
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 15 gms (0.5 oz) of dill, finely chopped
  • 2 x 400g (14.1 oz) tin of four bean mix
  • 1 red capsicum, diced
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 60 gms (2.1 oz) of kalamata olives, pitted
  • 1 x 400g (14.1 oz) tin of corn kernels, drained and rinsed

Method

  1. Season chicken with 5 mls (0.5 fl oz) of olive oil, paprika, garlic powder, oregano, 1 sprinkle of salt and pepper.
  2. Cook chicken in a frypan over medium-high heat for 5-6 minutes each side or until cooked through. Once cooked, shred and set aside.
  3. In a large mixing bowl, combine yoghurt with fetta, remaining olive oil, lemon juice, harissa paste, garlic and dill.
  4. Add the shredded chicken, four bean mix, red capsicum, green capsicum, onion, olives and corn. Season with salt and pepper and toss to combine.
  5. Divide into four serves and store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 500 gms (17.6 oz) of lean beef steak
