Cottage Cheese & Avo Toast

Serves 1

556Calories
22.25gProtein
30.48gFat
42.24gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 3 slices of wholegrain bread, toasted
  • 1/3 avocado, mashed
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of cottage cheese
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 1 tsp of chilli flakes
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil

Method

  1. Layer toast with avocado and cottage cheese. Sprinkle with salt, pepper and chilli flakes and drizzle with olive oil.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free bread
