Satay Chicken Soba Noodle Salad

Serves 2

622 kcalCalories
43.58 gProtein
21.8 gFat
58.51 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 1 x 165ml (5.6 fl oz) tin of reduced fat coconut milk
  • 30 mls (1 fl oz) of soy sauce
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 5 gms (0.2 oz) of ginger, grated or finely chopped
  • 250 gms (8.8 oz) of chicken breast, sliced
  • 130 gms (4.6 oz) of dry soba noodles
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of rice wine vinegar
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of honey
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of lime juice
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of peanut butter
  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of coleslaw cabbage mix
  • 1 spring onion bunch, sliced

Method

  1. Combine 60 mls (2 fl oz) of coconut milk, 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of soy sauce, garlic and ginger with chicken and marinate for 1 hour if time permits.
  2. Cook chicken in a frypan over medium-high heat for 8 minutes each side or until cooked through. Set aside to rest and then slice.
  3. Cook noodles according to packet instructions.
  4. Whisk together the remaining coconut milk, soy sauce, rice wine vinegar, honey, lime juice and peanut butter.
  5. Combine coleslaw cabbage mix with spring onion and soba noodles. Pour over the dressing and toss to combine.
  6. Divide soba noodle salad into two serves and top with chicken. Store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

*GF adaptation: ensure soba noodles are gluten free and use a gluten free soy sauce or tamari

**Suggested protein substitution: 210 gms (7.4 oz) of lean beef steak
