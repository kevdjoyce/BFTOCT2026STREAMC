Greek Salmon Salad

Serves 1

614 kcalCalories
48.05 gProtein
40.78 gFat
9.43 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 200 gms (8.1 oz) of salmon fillet
  • 2 sprinkles of salt
  • 2 sprinkles of pepper
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of lemon juice
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of iceberg lettuce, roughly chopped or shredded
  • 1/2 lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 1/4 red capsicum, diced
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of kalamata olives, pitted
  • 1/4 red onion, diced
  • 1/2 tsp of oregano

Method

  1. Season salmon with 1 sprinkle of salt, pepper, garlic, 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of lemon juice and 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) olive oil. Pan fry over medium-high heat for 4-5 minutes each side or until cooked to your liking.
  2. Combine lettuce, cucumber, capsicum, tomatoes, olives and red onion.
  3. Dress salad with remaining olive oil, lemon juice, salt and pepper and oregano.
  4. Flake salmon and serve on top of salad.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 350 gms (12.3 oz) of lean lamb fillet
