Turkey & Cottage Cheese Wraps

Serves 1

585 kcalCalories
30.41 gProtein
29.4 gFat
46.51 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of baby spinach
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of deli roast turkey slices
  • 1/3 tomato, sliced
  • 1/3 lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of cranberry sauce
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of cottage cheese
  • 1/3 avocado
  • 1 wholegrain wrap

Method

  1. Combine spinach, tomato and cucumber and dress with olive oil.
  2. Assemble salad into wrap with cranberry sauce, cottage cheese, avocado and turkey.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use a gluten free wrap
