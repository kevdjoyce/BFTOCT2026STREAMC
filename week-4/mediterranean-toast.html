Mediterranean Toast

Serves 1

631 kcalCalories
27.41 gProtein
34.63 gFat
46.15 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of kalamata olives, roughly chopped
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of roasted capsicum, drained and roughly chopped
  • 5 gms (0.2 oz) of baby capers
  • 5 gms (0.2 oz) of parsley, roughly chopped
  • 3 slices of wholegrain bread, toasted
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of hummus
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of fetta, crumbled

Method

  1. Combine olives, capers, roasted capsicum and parsley.
  2. Spread toast with hummus and top with Mediterranean mix and fetta.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free bread
