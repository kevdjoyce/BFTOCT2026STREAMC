Salmon & Fried Rice

Serves 1

635 kcalCalories
40.49 gProtein
25.8 gFat
54.88 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of basmati rice
  • 1/2 tsp of chicken stock powder
  • 150 gms (5.3 oz) of salmon fillet
  • 2 sprinkles of salt
  • 1/2 tsp of garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp of onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp of paprika
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 1/4 spring onion bunch, sliced
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of frozen peas

Method

  1. Cook rice according to packet instructions in added stock powder.
  2. Season salmon with salt, 1/4 tsp of garlic powder, 1/4 tsp of onion powder and 1/4 tsp of paprika.
  3. Heat a frypan over medium-high heat and cook salmon for 4-5 minutes each side or until cooked to your liking.
  4. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Add garlic, spring onion, carrot and peas and cook for 5 minutes.
  5. Add cooked rice into the pan with remaining garlic powder, onion powder and paprika and combine well. Heat for another 5-10 minutes or until vegetables are softened.
  6. Serve salmon on top of fried rice.

Notes

*Suggested protein substitution: 300 gms (10.6 oz) of chicken breast
