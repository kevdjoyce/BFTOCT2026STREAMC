Hoisin Beef Bowl

Serves 2

616 kcalCalories
35.19 gProtein
15.72 gFat
76.45 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 140 gms (4.9 oz) of basmati rice
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1 lebanese cucumber, julienned
  • 5 gms (0.2 oz) of chives, finely chopped
  • 15 gms (0.5 oz) of coriander, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp of chilli flakes
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 15 gms (0.5 oz) of brown sugar
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of sesame oil
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of white wine vinegar
  • 1 bok choy, leaves separated
  • 250 gms (8.8 oz) of lean beef mince
  • 1 tsp of chinese five spice
  • 30 mls (1 fl oz) of hoisin sauce
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of soy sauce
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of sesame seeds

Method

  1. Cook rice according to packet instructions.
  2. Combine carrot and cucumber with chives, coriander, 1/2 tsp chilli flakes, garlic, half the brown sugar, sesame oil and 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of white wine vinegar. Set aside.
  3. Steam bok choy.
  4. Heat a frypan over medium-high heat. Add mince meat and cook, breaking apart. Season with chinese five spice and 1/2 tsp of chilli flakes.
  5. Once the meat is browned, add hoisin sauce, soy sauce, 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of white wine vinegar and remaining brown sugar. Stir to combine and heat for another 4 minutes.
  6. Divide rice into two serves. Top with hoisin beef, cucumber and carrot salad and steamed bok choy. Sprinkle with sesame seeds. Store leftovers in the fridge, keeping the carrot and cucumber salad separate.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 250 gms (8.8 oz) of lean pork mince
