Greek Chicken 'Nachos'

Serves 2

623 kcalCalories
67.17 gProtein
13.93 gFat
48.53 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 400 gms (14.1 oz) of potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 1 red capsicum, thinly sliced
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 30 mls (1 fl oz) of lemon juice
  • 2 tsp of oregano
  • 1 tsp of garlic powder
  • 3 sprinkles of salt
  • 2 sprinkles of pepper
  • 400 gms (14.1 oz) of chicken breast, diced
  • 250 gms (8.8 oz) of high protein natural yoghurt
  • 1 lebanese cucumber, grated and excess water removed
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 15 gms (0.5 oz) of dill, finely chopped
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of baby spinach, roughly chopped
  • 5 gms (0.2 oz) of parsley, roughly chopped
  • 250 gms (8.8 oz) of cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 red onion, finely chopped

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200 degrees Celsius (400 F) and line a tray with baking paper.
  2. Combine potatoes and capsicum with 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of olive oil, 15 mls (0.5 fl oz) lemon juice, 1 tsp oregano, 1 tsp of garlic powder, salt and pepper. Transfer to baking tray and roast in the oven for 40 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, season chicken with 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of olive oil, 1 tsp of oregano, salt and pepper. Heat a frypan over medium-high heat and cook chicken for 5-8 minutes or until cooked through.
  4. Combine yoghurt with grated cucumber, 15 mls (0.5 fl oz) of lemon juice, garlic, dill, 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of olive oil and salt.
  5. When ready to serve, layer potato and capsicum with chicken, spinach, parsley, cherry tomatoes, red onion and homemade tzatziki.
  6. Store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 360 gms (12.7 oz) of lean lamb fillet
