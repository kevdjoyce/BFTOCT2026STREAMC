Garlic Chilli Noodles

Serves 2

621Calories
45.17gProtein
18.26gFat
58.72gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 250 gms (8.8 oz) of lean beef mince
  • 3/4 spring onion bunch, sliced
  • 1 red capsicum, sliced
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1 broccolini bunch, cut into thirds
  • 60 mls (2 fl oz) of soy sauce
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of rice wine vinegar
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of honey
  • 1 tsp of chilli flakes
  • 300 gms (10.6 oz) of fresh udon noodles
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of sesame seeds

Method

  1. Heat oil in a large fry pan over medium-high heat. Add brown onion and garlic and sauté for 1-2 minutes.
  2. Add mince and cook, breaking apart, for 5 minutes.
  3. Add spring onion, capsicum, carrot and broccolini and cook for 5-6 minutes or until vegetables start to soften.
  4. Add soy sauce, rice wine vinegar, honey and chilli flakes along with udon noodles and cook for another 5 minutes.
  5. Divide noodles into two serves and store leftovers in the fridge. When ready to serve, sprinkle with sesame seeds.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use a gluten free rice noodle

**Suggested protein substitution: 250 gms (8.8 oz) of lean pork mince
