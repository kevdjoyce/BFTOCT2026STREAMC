Cereal & Peaches

Serves 1

574Calories
37.07gProtein
7.77gFat
79.12gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of whey protein powder
  • 300 mls (10.1 oz) of reduced fat milk
  • 80 gms (2.8 oz) of no added sugar wheat flakes
  • 150 gms (5.3 oz) of canned peaches in natural juice, drained

Method

  1. Combine the protein powder and milk and mix until well combined.
  2. Pour over cereal and top with peaches.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use a gluten free flake cereal
