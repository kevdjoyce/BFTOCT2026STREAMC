Lemon Chicken & Sesame Cabbage Salad

Serves 4

600Calories
50.78gProtein
19.11gFat
50.05gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 700 gms (24.7 oz) of chicken breast
  • 30 mls (1 fl oz) of lemon juice
  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of brown rice
  • 1 cabbage, sliced thinly or shredded
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • 1 lebanese cucumber, sliced thinly into half moons
  • 40 gms (1.4 oz) of peanuts, roughly chopped
  • 5 gms (0.2 oz) of parsley, roughly chopped
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of chives, roughly chopped
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of sesame seeds
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of white wine vinegar
  • 30 mls (1 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 2 tsp of chilli flakes

Method

  1. Poach chicken with an added 15 mls (0.5 fl oz) of lemon juice. Shred.
  2. Meanwhile, cook rice according to packet instructions.
  3. Combine cooked chicken and rice with cabbage, onion, cucumber, peanuts, parsley, chives and sesame seeds. Dress with remaining lemon juice, white wine vinegar, olive oil, salt, pepper and chilli flakes. Toss to combine.
  4. Divide into four serves and store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 590 gms (20.8 oz) of lean beef steak
