Egg & Cottage Cheese Sandwich

Serves 1

607Calories
45.2gProtein
23.31gFat
49.12gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 2 slices of wholegrain bread
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of reduced fat mayonnaise
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of cottage cheese
  • 1/2 tsp of mixed herbs
  • 1/2 tomato, sliced
  • 1/4 red onion, sliced
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of baby spinach
  • 250 mls (8.5 fl oz) of reduced fat milk

Method

  1. Fry eggs in a non-stick pan and cook to your liking.
  2. Layer sandwich with mayonnaise and cottage cheese. Sprinkle with mixed herbs. Top with eggs, tomato, onion and spinach. Serve with a glass of milk.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free bread
