Oregano Baked Fish

Serves 1

631Calories
46.98gProtein
17.47gFat
67.18gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 80 gms (2.8 oz) of basmati rice
  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of white fish fillet
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 1/2 tsp of oregano
  • 1/4 tsp of paprika
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 zucchini, sliced
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of kalamata olives, pitted

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 190 degrees Celsius (370 F) and prepare a baking dish.
  2. Cook rice according to packet instructions.
  3. Place fish in the baking dish.
  4. In a small bowl, combine the olive oil, garlic, oregano, paprika, salt and pepper. Pour over the fish.
  5. Scatter the cherry tomatoes, zucchini and olives around the fish.
  6. Bake in the oven for 20-25 minutes or until the fish is cooked through.
  7. Remove from oven and serve on top of rice.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 200 gms (7.1 oz) of chicken breast
