Chopped Roast Veg & Chickpeas With Haloumi

Serves 2

613Calories
31.32gProtein
23.42gFat
55.8gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 2 red capsicums, diced
  • 4 tomatoes, quartered
  • 3/4 red onion, diced into large cubes
  • 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 60 mls (2 fl oz) of balsamic vinegar
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 1 x 400g (14.1 oz) tin of chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 2 tsp of paprika
  • 90 gms (3.2 oz) of haloumi, sliced
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of parsley, finely chopped
  • 2 slices of wholegrain bread, toasted

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 220 degrees Celsius (430 F) and prepare two ovenproof dishes.
  2. Combine capsicum, tomatoes, onion and garlic with balsamic vinegar, 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of olive oil, salt and pepper. Transfer to a baking dish and roast in the oven for 45 minutes or until vegetables are very soft.
  3. Combine chickpeas with remaining olive oil and paprika. Add to second baking dish and roast in the oven for 20-30 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile, cook haloumi slices in a pan over medium-high heat for 2-3 minutes each side or until golden.
  5. When vegetables and chickpeas are done, remove from oven. Chop the vegetables finely and mix with chickpeas and parsley.
  6. Divide into two serves and top with haloumi. Serve alongside a slice of toast.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free bread

**Suggested protein substitution: 220 gms (7.8 oz) of chicken breast
