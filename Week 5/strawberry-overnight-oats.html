Strawberry Overnight Oats

Serves 4

604Calories
44.39gProtein
22.87gFat
46.79gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 500 gms (17.6 oz) of strawberries
  • 250 mls (8.5 fl oz) of reduced fat milk
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of whey protein powder
  • 500 gms (17.6 oz) of high protein natural yoghurt
  • 165 gms (5.8 oz) of rolled oats
  • 55 gms (1.9 oz) of chia seeds
  • 1 tsp of vanilla extract
  • 60 gms (2.1 oz) of shredded coconut
  • 40 gms (1.4 oz) of dark chocolate chips

Method

  1. Blitz together the strawberries, milk and protein powder in a blender until smooth.
  2. Mix with yoghurt, rolled oats, chia seeds and vanilla extract.
  3. Divide into four serves and allow to set in the fridge.
  4. When ready to serve, top with 15 gms (0.5 oz) of coconut and 10 gms (0.4 oz) of dark chocolate chips.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use quinoa flakes or brown rice flakes instead of rolled oats
