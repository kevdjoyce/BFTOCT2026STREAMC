Banana Berry Smoothie Bowl

Serves 1

439 kcalCalories
40.42 gProtein
9.76 gFat
37.88 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 160 g frozen mixed berries
  • 1 banana
  • 125 ml reduced fat milk
  • 40 g whey protein powder
  • 20 g chia seeds

Method

  1. Add frozen berries and 1/2 banana to a blender and blend on low until small bits remain.
  2. Add milk and protein powder, and blend on low again, scraping down sides as needed, until the mixture reaches a soft serve consistency.
  3. Scoop into serving bowl and top with the remaining banana and chia seeds.
