Chicken & Pesto Rice

Serves 4

624Calories
43.12gProtein
19.99gFat
64.27gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 red onion, diced
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 1 red capsicum, diced into large cubes
  • 2 zucchinis, diced
  • 600 gms (21.2 oz) of chicken breast, diced
  • 150 gms (5.3 oz) of basil pesto
  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of cherry tomatoes
  • 1 ltrs (33.8 fl oz) of chicken stock
  • 270 gms (9.5 oz) of basmati rice

Method

  1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add onion and garlic and sauté for 3-4 minutes.
  2. Add capsicum and zucchini and cook for 5 minutes or until vegetables start to soften.
  3. Add chicken and cook for another 5 minutes.
  4. Add the cherry tomatoes and pesto sauce and stir to combine.
  5. Pour in the stock and rice. Stir to combine. Cover and simmer for 20 minutes or until rice has absorbed liquid and is cooked.
  6. Divide into two serves and store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 500 gms (17.6 oz) of lean beef steak
