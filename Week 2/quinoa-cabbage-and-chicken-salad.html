Quinoa, Cabbage & Chicken Salad

Serves 2

614Calories
53.99gProtein
15.33gFat
59.51gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 300 gms (10.6 oz) of chicken breast
  • 150 gms (5.3 oz) of quinoa
  • 75 gms (2.6 oz) of edamame beans
  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of coleslaw cabbage mix
  • 3/4 spring onion bunch, sliced
  • 1 lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of cashews, roughly chopped
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of soy sauce
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of rice wine vinegar
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of honey
  • 20 mls 0.7 fl oz) of lime juice

Method

  1. Poach chicken and then shred.
  2. Cook quinoa and edamame beans according to packet instructions.
  3. Combine cooked quinoa and chicken with coleslaw cabbage mix, edamame beans, spring onion, cucumber and cashews.
  4. Combine soy sauce, rice wine vinegar, honey and lime juice. Drizzle over salad and toss to combine.
  5. Divide into two serves and store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free soy sauce or tamari

**Suggested protein substitution: 250 gms (8.8 oz) of lean beef steak
