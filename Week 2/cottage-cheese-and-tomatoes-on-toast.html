Cottage Cheese & Tomatoes On Toast

Serves 1

637Calories
50.52gProtein
26.38gFat
42.93gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 3 slices of wholegrain bread, toasted
  • 125 gms (4.4 oz) of cottage cheese
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 3 eggs, boiled

Method

  1. Layer toast with cottage cheese and tomatoes. Season with salt and pepper. Serve with boiled eggs.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free bread
