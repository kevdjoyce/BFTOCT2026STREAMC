Ham & Lettuce Sandwich

Serves 1

589Calories
43.72gProtein
20.76gFat
46.67gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 3 slices of wholegrain bread
  • 1/4 avocado
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of lean ham
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of reduced fat tasty cheese
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of iceberg lettuce
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 1/4 red onion, sliced

Method

  1. Spread bread with avocado and layer ham, cheese and salad into 1.5 sandwiches.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free bread

**Suggested protein substitution: 110 gms (3.9 oz) of chicken breast
