Greek Haloumi Bowls

Serves 2

709Calories
31.11gProtein
22.8gFat
88.65gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 160 gms (5.6 oz) of brown rice
  • 90 gms (3.2 oz) of haloumi, sliced
  • 150 gms (5.3 oz) of iceberg lettuce, roughly chopped or shredded
  • 1 lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of kalamata olives, pitted
  • 1/4 red onion, sliced
  • 15 mls (0.5 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of lemon juice
  • 1 tsp of oregano
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper

Tzatziki

  • 250 gms (8.8 oz) of high protein natural yoghurt
  • 1/2 lebanese cucumber, grated
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of lemon juice
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper

Method

  1. Cook rice according to packet instructions.
  2. Heat a pan over medium-high heat. Add haloumi slices and fry for 2 minutes each side or until browned.
  3. Combine lettuce, cucumber, tomatoes, olives and onion. Dress with olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, salt and pepper. Toss to combine.
  4. Combine tzatziki ingredients and mix well.
  5. To assemble, layer bowls with rice and top with haloumi, greek salad and homemade tzatziki.
  6. Store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 220 gms (7.8 oz) of chicken breast
