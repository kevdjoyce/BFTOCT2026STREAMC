Salmon, Edamame & Soba Noodle Salad

Serves 1

611Calories
38.3gProtein
21.39gFat
62.73gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of salmon fillet
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of soy sauce
  • 70 gms (2.5 oz) of soba noodles
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of edamame beans
  • 1/2 lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 1/4 spring onion bunch, sliced
  • 1/4 avocado, diced

Dressing

  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of reduced fat mayonnaise
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of soy sauce
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of sriracha
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of water, to thin

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180 degrees Celsius (350 F) and line a tray with baking paper.
  2. Combine salmon with soy sauce and transfer to baking tray. Roast in the oven for 20-25 minutes. Remove and set aside.
  3. Cook noodles and edamame according to packet instructions.
  4. Combine noodles and edamame beans with cucumber, spring onion and avocado.
  5. Combine dressing ingredients and drizzle over noodle salad. Toss to combine and top with salmon.

Notes

*GF adaptation: ensure noodles are gluten free and use a gluten free soy sauce or tamari

**Suggested protein substitution: 170 gms (6 oz) of lean beef steak
