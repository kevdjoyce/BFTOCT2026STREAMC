Haloumi & Sweet Potato Nourish Bowls

Serves 2

628Calories
21.63gProtein
38.33gFat
42.67gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 400 gms (14.1 oz) of sweet potato, peeled and diced
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 90 gms (3.2 oz) of haloumi, sliced
  • 60 gms (2.1 oz) of baby spinach
  • 1 x 450g (15.9 oz) tin of baby beetroot, drained and halved
  • 1/2 red onion, diced
  • 60 gms (2.1 oz) of walnuts, roughly chopped
  • 15 gms (0.5 oz) of parsley, finely chopped

Dressing

  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of balsamic vinegar
  • 5 gms (0.2 oz) of dijon mustard
  • 1/2 tsp of garlic powder
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200 degrees Celsius (400 F) and line a tray with baking paper.
  2. Toss sweet potato in olive oil and roast for 35 minutes or until soft and caramelised.
  3. Heat pan over medium heat and panfry haloumi slices for 2 minutes each side or until browned.
  4. Combine dressing ingredients.
  5. Assemble nourish bowl- layer spinach with sweet potato, beetroot, red onion, haloumi and walnuts. Sprinkle with parsley and drizzle with dressing.
  6. Store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 220 gms (7.8 oz) of chicken breast
