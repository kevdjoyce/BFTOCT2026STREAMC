Beef & Broccoli

Serves 2

613 kcalCalories
42.51 gProtein
7.45 gFat
86.15 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 250 gms (8.8 oz) of lean beef steak, sliced
  • 1/2 tsp of bicarbonate soda
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of mirin
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of soy sauce
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of oyster sauce
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of water
  • 2 tsp of cornstarch
  • 160 gms (5.6 oz) of basmati rice
  • 2 broccoli heads, cut into smaller florets
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of sesame oil
  • 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of ginger, grated or finely chopped

Stir Fry Sauce

  • 30 mls (1 fl oz) of oyster sauce
  • 40 mls (1.4 fl oz) of soy sauce
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of mirin
  • 125 mls (4.2 fl oz) of chicken stock
  • 2 tsp of cornstarch

Method

  1. Combine beef with bicarbonate soda, mirin, soy sauce, oyster sauce, water and cornstarch. Set aside to marinate for 15 minutes or longer.
  2. Mix together the stir fry sauce ingredients. Set aside.
  3. Cook rice according to packet instructions.
  4. Bring a pot of water to the boil. Add broccoli and cook for 2 minutes. Drain and set aside.
  5. Heat sesame oil in a large frypan or wok over medium-high heat. Add garlic and ginger and stirfry for 1 minute.
  6. Add broccoli and cook for another minute.
  7. Add the beef and cook for 5 minutes. Add the stir fry sauce and toss together for 1-2 minutes or until the sauce is thickened and evenly coated.
  8. Divide rice into two serves and top with beef and broccoli. Store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free soy sauce or tamari. Ensure oyster sauce and cornstarch are gluten free.

**Suggested protein substitution: 300 gms (10.6 oz) of lean pork fillet
