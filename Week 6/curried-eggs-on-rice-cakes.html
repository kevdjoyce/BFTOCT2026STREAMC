Curried Eggs On Rice Cakes

Serves 1

598Calories
34.13gProtein
31.87gFat
40.21gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 5 eggs, boiled
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of reduced fat mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp of curry powder
  • 6 multigrain rice cakes
  • 1/2 avocado
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of baby spinach
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 1/4 lebanese cucumber, sliced

Method

  1. Smash the eggs with mayonnaise and curry powder.
  2. Layer rice cakes with avocado, spinach, tomato, cucumber and curried eggs.
