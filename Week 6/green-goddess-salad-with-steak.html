Green Goddess Salad With Steak

Serves 2

616Calories
53.46gProtein
27.28gFat
33.98gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 300 gms (10.6 oz) of lean beef steak
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of edamame beans
  • 1 x 400g (14.1 oz) tin of chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1/2 lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 40 gms (1.4 oz) of baby spinach

Dressing

  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of cashews
  • 15 gms (0.5 oz) of coriander
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of baby spinach
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of honey
  • 25 mls (0.8 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 80 mls (2.7 fl oz) of water
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of lemon juice

Method

  1. Season steaks with salt and pepper. Cook in a frypan over medium-high heat for 2-3 minutes each side or until cooked to your liking. Set aside to cool and then slice.
  2. Cook edamame beans according to packet instructions.
  3. Combine edamame beans with chickpeas, cucumber and spinach.
  4. Add dressing ingredients to a blender or food processor and blitz until smooth.
  5. Divide into two serves and store leftovers in the fridge.
  6. When ready to serve, toss salad with dressing and top with steak.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 320 gms (11.3 oz) of lean lamb fillet
