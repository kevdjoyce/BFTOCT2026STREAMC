Salmon Crispy Rice Salad

Serves 1

622Calories
42.4gProtein
26.41gFat
52.25gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of basmati rice
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of soy sauce
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of sesame oil
  • 150 gms (5.3 oz) of salmon fillet
  • 1/4 tsp of garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp of paprika
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of edamame beans
  • 1/4 lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of reduced fat mayonnaise
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of high protein natural yoghurt
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of light sweet chilli sauce

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200 degrees Celsius (400 F) and line a tray with baking paper.
  2. Cook rice according to packet instructions.
  3. Toss cooked rice with 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of soy sauce and sesame oil.
  4. Transfer to baking tray and spread in an even layer. Bake in the oven for 30 minutes or until crisp.
  5. Season salmon with 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of soy sauce, garlic powder, paprika and salt.
  6. Cook salmon in a nonstick frypan over medium-high heat for 3-4 minutes each side until cooked to your liking.
  7. Cook edamame beans according to packet instructions.
  8. Combine crispy rice with cucumber and edamame beans. Toss with mayonnaise, yoghurt and sweet chilli sauce.
  9. Top rice salad with salmon and serve.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free soy sauce or tamari

**Suggested protein substitution: 250 gms (8.8 oz) of lean beef steak
