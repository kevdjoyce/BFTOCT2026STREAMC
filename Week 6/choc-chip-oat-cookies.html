Choc Chip Oat Cookies

Serves 12

141Calories
5.01gProtein
6.14gFat
15.35gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 2 bananas, mashed
  • 125 gms (4.4 oz) of rolled oats
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of peanut butter
  • 120 gms (4.2 oz) of dark chocolate chips
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of whey protein powder

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180 degrees Celsius (350 F) and line a tray with baking paper.
  2. Mix all ingredients together in a bowl until well combined. Shape the dough into 12 cookies and place on baking tray.
  3. Bake in the oven for 15-20 minutes. Allow to cool for 5 minutes.
  4. Store in an airtight container in the fridge. One serve is one cookie. We will have these over two weeks.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use quinoa flakes or brown rice flakes instead of rolled oats.
