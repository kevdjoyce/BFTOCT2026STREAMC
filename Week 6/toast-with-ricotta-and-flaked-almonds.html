Toast With Ricotta & Flaked Almonds

Serves 1

600Calories
27.79gProtein
27.59gFat
54.43gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 125 gms (4.4 oz) of ricotta
  • 2 slices of wholegrain bread, toasted
  • 1 banana, sliced
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of flaked almonds
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of honey

Method

  1. Spread ricotta on toast and top with sliced banana and flaked almonds. Drizzle with honey.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free bread
