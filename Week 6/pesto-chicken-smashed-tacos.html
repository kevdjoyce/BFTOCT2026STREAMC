Pesto Chicken Smashed Tacos

Serves 2

602Calories
41.55gProtein
25.91gFat
47.9gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 250 gms (8.8 oz) of chicken mince
  • 40 gms (1.4 oz) of basil pesto
  • 6 mini tortillas (~25g ((0.8 oz)) each)
  • 60 gms (2.1 oz) of reduced fat tasty cheese
  • 40 gms (1.4 oz) of baby spinach
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of cherry tomatoes, quartered
  • 1/4 red onion, diced
  • 1/2 lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of balsamic glaze

Method

  1. Combine chicken mince with pesto.
  2. Lay out mini tortillas and spread a layer of the chicken mixture directly onto one side of each tortilla, pressing it down firmly so it sticks.
  3. Heat a pan over medium-high heat. Place tortilla in the pan, chicken side down and cook for 5-6 minutes or until chicken is golden and cooked through and tortilla is crisp underneath.
  4. Flip the tortilla and add cheese. Allow to melt and then remove from pan.
  5. Divide into two serves. Layer chicken tortilla with spinach, tomatoes, onion and cucumber. Drizzle with balsamic glaze.
  6. Store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free corn mini tortillas

**Suggested protein substitution: 250 gms (8.8 oz) of turkey mince
