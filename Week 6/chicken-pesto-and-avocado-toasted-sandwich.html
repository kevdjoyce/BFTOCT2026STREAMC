Chicken, Pesto & Avocado Toasted Sandwich

Serves 1

631Calories
48.63gProtein
27.17gFat
42.6gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 3 slices of wholegrain bread
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of chicken breast
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of reduced fat tasty cheese
  • 1/4 avocado
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of basil pesto
  • 15 gms (0.5 oz) of baby spinach
  • 1/2 tomato, sliced

Method

  1. Cook chicken slices in a nonstick pan over medium-high heat for 5-8 minutes or until cooked through.
  2. Slice one of the breads in half. Spread 1.5 slices of bread with basil pesto and the other 1.5 slices with avocado. Assemble 1.5 sandwiches with cheese, chicken, spinach and tomato. Toast in a sandwich press, grill or pan until bread is crispy and cheese has melted.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free bread

**Suggested protein substitution: 1 x 95g (3.4 oz) tin of tuna in springwater
