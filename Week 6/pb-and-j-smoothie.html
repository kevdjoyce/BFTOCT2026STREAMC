PB & J Smoothie

Serves 1

609Calories
46.03gProtein
31.27gFat
29.77gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 300 mls (10.1 fl oz) of reduced fat milk
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of whey protein powder
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of peanut butter
  • 165 gms (5.8 oz) of frozen strawberries
  • 15 gms (0.5 oz) of chia seeds
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of shredded coconut

Method

  1. Blitz all ingredients together in a high speed blender until smooth.
