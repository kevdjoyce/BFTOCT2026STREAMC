Chilli Chicken Loaded Baked Potato

Serves 2

606 kcalCalories
70.25 gProtein
11.1 gFat
46.28 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 400 gms (14.1 oz) of potatoes
  • 400 gms (14.1 oz) of chicken breast
  • 1 x 400g (14.1 oz) tin of diced tomatoes
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of tomato paste
  • 300 mls (10.1 fl oz) of chicken stock
  • 1 tsp of chilli powder
  • 1 tsp of paprika
  • 1 tsp of mixed herbs
  • 1/2 tsp of garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp of onion powder
  • 1/2 red capsicum, diced
  • 1 green capsicum, diced
  • 1/4 x 400g (14.1 oz) tin of black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 80 gms (2.8 oz) of reduced fat tasty cheese, grated

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200 degrees Celsius (400 F) and line a tray with baking paper.
  2. Pierce potatoes with a fork and add to baking tray. Bake in the oven for 45 minutes or until tender.
  3. Add chicken, tinned tomatoes, tomato paste, stock, chilli powder, paprika, mixed herbs, garlic powder and onion powder to a saucepan. Cover with a lid and simmer saucepan on medium-low for 40-50 minutes, turning the chicken occasionally.
  4. Once chicken is cooked, remove from saucepan and shred. Add shredded chicken to a separate frypan over medium-high heat and add capsicums and black beans. Cook for 5 minutes or until capsicum has softened. Then, add chicken and capsicum back to the sauce and mix to combine.
  5. Once potatoes are cooked, cut them open and add cheese. Top with chicken mixture.
  6. Store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 340 gms (12 oz) of lean beef steak
