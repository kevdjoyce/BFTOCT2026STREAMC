BBQ Chicken Bowl

Serves 1

612 kcalCalories
56.84 gProtein
14.81 gFat
54.91 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 40 gms (1.4 oz) of brown rice
  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of chicken breast
  • 70 gms (2.5 oz) of sweet potato, peeled and diced
  • 1/2 red capsicum, sliced into two slices
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tsp of paprika
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 1/4 x 400g (14.1 oz) tin of black beans, drained and rinsed

Method

  1. Cook rice according to packet instructions.
  2. Combine chicken, sweet potato and capsicum with olive oil, salt and paprika.
  3. Grill vegetables and chicken on the bbq over high heat for 5-7 minutes each side or until browned and cooked through. If unable to use the bbq, panfry instead.
  4. Divide rice, chicken, sweet potato, capsicum and black beans into two serves. Store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 170 gms (6 oz) of lean lamb fillet
