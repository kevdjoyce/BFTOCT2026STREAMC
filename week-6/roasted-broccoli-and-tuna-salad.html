Roasted Broccoli & Tuna Salad

Serves 4

600 kcalCalories
36.64 gProtein
43.58 gFat
10.25 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 2 broccoli heads, cut into smaller florets
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 x 400g (14.1 oz) tin of cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1/2 red onion, diced
  • 150 gms (5.3 oz) of cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 15 gms (0.5 oz) of parsley, finely chopped
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of flaked almonds
  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of fetta, crumbled
  • 1 x 425g (15 oz) tin of tuna in olive oil, drained

Dressing

  • 50 mls (1.7 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 30 mls (1 fl oz) of lemon juice
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of dijon mustard
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200 degrees Celsius (400 F) and line a tray with baking paper.
  2. Toss broccoli florets with olive oil and salt. Transfer to baking tray and roast for 20-25 minutes or until tender and lightly browned.
  3. Combine roasted broccoli with beans, onion, cherry tomatoes, parsley, almonds, fetta and tuna.
  4. Combine dressing ingredients and pour over the salad. Toss to combine.
  5. Divide into four serves and store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 600 gms (21.2 oz) of chicken breast
