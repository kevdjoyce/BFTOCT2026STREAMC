Creamy Chicken Pasta Salad

Serves 4

617Calories
58.81gProtein
8.55gFat
70.26gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 350 gms (12.3 oz) of pasta
  • 700 gms (24.7 oz) of chicken breast
  • 15 mls (0.5 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/4 tsp of garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp of paprika
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 1 broccoli, finely chopped
  • 1 red capsicum, diced
  • 1 lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of coleslaw cabbage mix

Dressing

  • 150 gms (5.3 oz) of high protein natural yoghurt
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of reduced fat mayonnaise
  • 15 gms (0.5 oz) of dijon mustard
  • 25 mls (0.8 fl oz) of lemon juice
  • 15 gms (0.5 oz) of dill, finely chopped
  • 70 mls (2.4 fl oz) of water
  • 1/2 tsp of garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp of onion powder

Method

  1. Cook pasta according to packet instructions.
  2. Whisk dressing ingredients together.
  3. Toss chicken with olive oil, garlic powder, paprika, salt and pepper. Cook chicken in a frypan over medium-high heat for 5 minutes each side or until cooked through. Once cooked, shred.
  4. In a large salad bowl, add cooked pasta, chicken, broccoli, red capsicum, cucumber and coleslaw mix. Pour over the dressing and toss to combine.
  5. Divide into four serves and store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free pasta

**Suggested protein substitution: 590 gms (20.8 oz) of lean beef steak
