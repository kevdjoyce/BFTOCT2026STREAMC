Beef & Avocado Fajita Wraps

Serves 2

605Calories
54.3gProtein
21.96gFat
43.96gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 300 gms (10.6 oz) of lean beef steak, sliced
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of fajita seasoning
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of baby spinach
  • 1/2 lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of cherry tomatoes, quartered
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of lemon juice
  • 2 wholegrain wraps
  • 40 gms (1.4 oz) of reduced fat tasty cheese, grated
  • 1/2 avocado, sliced
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of high protein natural yoghurt

Method

  1. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Add beef slices and cook for 5-6 minutes or until cooked through. Add fajita seasoning and stir to combine.
  2. Combine spinach, cucumber and tomatoes. Dress with lemon juice.
  3. To serve, assemble wrap with cheese, beef slices, avocado, salad and yoghurt. Store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free wraps

**Suggested protein substitution: 350 gms (12.3 oz) of chicken breast
