Toast With Cheese & Tomato

Serves 1

611Calories
43.57gProtein
19.97gFat
56.88gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 3 slices of wholegrain bread, toasted
  • 60 gms (2.1 oz) of reduced fat tasty cheese
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 200 mls (6.8 fl oz) of reduced fat milk

Method

  1. Top toast with cheese, sliced tomatoes, salt and pepper. Serve with a glass of milk.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free bread
