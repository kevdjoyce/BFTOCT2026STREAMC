Roasted Capsicum & White Beans In Red Sauce

Serves 2

617Calories
18.66gProtein
10.86gFat
99.18gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 180 gms (6.3 oz) of brown rice
  • 15 mls (0.5 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 1 tsp of paprika
  • 1 tsp of cumin
  • 80 gms (2.8 oz) of roasted capsicum, drained and roughly chopped
  • 1 x 400g (14.1 oz) tin of diced tomatoes
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 x 400g (14.1 oz) tin of cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1/2 lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of parsley, finely chopped
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of lemon juice

Method

  1. Cook rice according to packet instructions.
  2. Heat 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of oil in a frypan over medium-high heat. Add onion, garlic, paprika and cumin and sauté for 3 minutes.
  3. Add roasted capsicum and heat for 3 minutes.
  4. Add diced tomatoes, salt and beans and cook for 10 minutes or until warmed through.
  5. Combine cucumber, tomatoes, parsley, lemon juice and olive oil.
  6. Divide rice and beans into two serves. When ready to serve, top with salad.
  7. Store leftovers in the fridge.
