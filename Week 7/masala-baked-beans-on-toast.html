Masala Baked Beans On Toast

Serves 2

615Calories
34.33gProtein
18.52gFat
66.01gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 1 tsp of cumin
  • 1/2 tsp of garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp of turmeric
  • 1 x 400g (14.1 oz) tin of four bean mix, drained and rinsed
  • 1 x 400g (14.1 oz) tin of diced tomatoes
  • 6 slices of wholegrain bread, toasted
  • 60 gms (2.1 oz) of reduced fat tasty cheese, grated

Method

  1. Heat oil in a frypan over medium-high heat. Add onion and sauté for 2 minutes.
  2. Add spices and stir to combine. Heat for 1 minute.
  3. Add the beans and diced tomatoes. Turn the heat down and simmer for 10 minutes or until warmed through.
  4. Divide into two serves and store leftovers in the fridge. When ready to serve, top toast with beans and sprinkle with cheese.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free bread
