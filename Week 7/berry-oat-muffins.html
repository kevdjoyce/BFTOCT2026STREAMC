Berry Oat Muffins

Serves 12

121Calories
4.91gProtein
3.5gFat
16.32gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 2 bananas, mashed
  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of high protein natural yoghurt
  • 65 mls (2.2 fl oz) of reduced fat milk
  • 50 mls (1.7 fl oz) of honey
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp of vanilla extract
  • 130 gms (4.6 oz) of rolled oats
  • 1 tsp of cinnamon
  • 1 tsp of baking powder
  • 125 gms (4.4 oz) of raspberries
  • 35 gms (1.2 oz) of walnuts, roughly chopped

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 190 degrees Celsius (370 F) and prepare a 12-cup muffin pan.
  2. Combine mashed banana, yoghurt, milk, honey, egg and vanilla extract. Whisk until smooth.
  3. Stir in the rolled oats, cinnamon and baking powder. Gently fold in the berries.
  4. Divide mixture evenly between 12 muffin cups.
  5. Sprinkle walnuts on top of each muffin.
  6. Bake for 30 minutes or until lightly golden. Allow to cool for at least 30 minutes.
  7. Store in an airtight container in the fridge or freezer. We will have these over two weeks.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use quinoa flakes or brown rice flakes.
