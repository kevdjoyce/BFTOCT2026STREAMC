Banana & Pecan Chia Pudding

Serves 4

616Calories
37.04gProtein
31.36gFat
40.13gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 500 mls (16.9 fl oz) of reduced fat milk
  • 120 gms (4.2 oz) of whey protein powder
  • 5 bananas
  • 1/2 tsp of cinnamon
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of maple syrup
  • 1/2 tsp of vanilla extract
  • 90 gms (3.2 oz) of chia seeds
  • 120 gms (4.2 oz) of pecans

Method

  1. Blend together the milk, protein powder, 1 banana, cinnamon, maple syrup and vanilla extract.
  2. Transfer to a bowl and add chia seeds. Combine well with a whisk or fork. Allow to set for 5 minutes before mixing again to help avoid mixture from forming clumps.
  3. Divide into four serves and store in the fridge.
  4. When ready to serve, top with a sliced banana and 30 gms (1.1 oz) of pecans.
