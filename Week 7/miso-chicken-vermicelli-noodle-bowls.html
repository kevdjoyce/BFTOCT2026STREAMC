Miso Chicken Vermicelli Noodle Bowls

Serves 2

608Calories
45.68gProtein
11.21gFat
78.4gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 350 gms (12.3 oz) of chicken breast, diced
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of miso paste
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of soy sauce
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of rice vinegar
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of honey
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of sesame oil
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 5 gms (0.2 oz) of ginger, grated or finely chopped
  • 140 gms (4.9 oz) of vermicelli noodles
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of edamame beans
  • 2/3 lebanese cucumber, julienned
  • 1/2 carrot, julienned or grated

Dressing

  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of miso paste
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of soy sauce
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of rice vinegar
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of honey
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of sesame oil
  • 5 gms (0.2 oz) of ginger, grated or finely chopped

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken- in a bowl, whisk together the miso paste, soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey, sesame oil, garlic and ginger. Add chicken and toss to coat evenly. Marinate for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat a pan over medium-high heat and cook chicken for 5-8 minutes or until cooked through.
  3. Cook noodles and edamame beans according to packet instructions.
  4. Whisk together the dressing ingredients until smooth.
  5. Divide noodles into two serves. Top with chicken, edamame beans, cucumber and carrots. Drizzle with dressing.
  6. Store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

*GF adaptation: ensure noodles are gluten free and use gluten free soy sauce or tamari

**Suggested protein substitution: 350 gms (12.3 oz) of lean pork fillet
