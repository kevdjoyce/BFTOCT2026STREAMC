Lamb Pitas With Tahini Sauce

Serves 2

622Calories
46.33gProtein
17.03gFat
63.33gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 170 gms (6 oz) of high protein natural yoghurt
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of tahini
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of lemon juice
  • 3 sprinkles of salt
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of honey
  • 30 mls (1 fl oz) of water
  • 1/2 lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of cherry tomatoes, quartered
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of baby spinach, roughly chopped
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 250 gms (8.8 oz) of lamb backstrap, sliced thinly
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tsp of paprika
  • 2 tsp of cumin
  • 15 gms (0.5 oz) of parsley, finely chopped
  • 6 mini wholemeal pita bread (~35g ((1.2 oz)) each)

Method

  1. Mix yoghurt with tahini, 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of lemon juice, 1 sprinkle of salt, honey and water until smooth.
  2. Combine cucumber, tomatoes and spinach. Sprinkle with salt and pepper and toss with remaining lemon juice.
  3. Combine lamb slices with olive oil, paprika, cumin, salt and parsley. Cook in a pan over medium-high heat for 3-5 minutes or until cooked through.
  4. To serve, top 3 mini pita breads with lamb, salad and tahini sauce. Store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free pita pockets

**Suggested protein substitution: 230 gms (8.1 oz) of lean beef steak
