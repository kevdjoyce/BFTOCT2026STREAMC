Quick Salmon Poke Bowl

Serves 1

608Calories
34.04gProtein
20.37gFat
67.79gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 1 x 125g (4.4 oz) microwavable brown rice cup
  • 70 gms (2.5 oz) of edamame beans
  • 1 x 105g (3.7 oz) tin of pink salmon, drained
  • 1/2 lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • 1/2 carrot, julienned or grated
  • 1/4 avocado, diced
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of reduced fat mayonnaise
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of sesame seeds

Method

  1. Cook rice and edamame beans according to packet instructions.
  2. Assemble poke bowl with salmon, edamame, cucumber, carrot and avocado. Drizzle with mayonnaise and sprinkle with sesame seeds.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 1 x 95g (3.4 oz) tin of tuna in springwater
