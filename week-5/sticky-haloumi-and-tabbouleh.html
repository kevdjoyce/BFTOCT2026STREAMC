Sticky Haloumi & Tabbouleh

Serves 2

607 kcalCalories
22.89 gProtein
19.53 gFat
83.09 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 190 gms (6.7 oz) of pearl couscous
  • 1 tsp of chicken stock powder
  • 90 gms (3.2 oz) of haloumi, diced into cubes
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of honey
  • 1/2 tsp of chilli flakes
  • 20 mls of extra virgin olive oil
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of parsley, finely chopped
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of baby spinach, roughly chopped
  • 3/4 lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 red onion, diced
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of lemon juice
  • 5 gms (0.2 oz) of dijon mustard

Method

  1. Cook couscous according to packet instructions in added stock powder.
  2. Combine haloumi with honey and chilli flakes.
  3. Heat 5 mls of olive oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Add haloumi and cook for 4-5 minutes or until golden brown.
  4. Combine couscous with parsley, spinach, cucumber, tomatoes and red onion.
  5. Combine remaining olive oil with lemon juice and dijon mustard. Pour dressing over tabbouleh and toss to combine.
  6. Divide tabbouleh into two serves and top with haloumi.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use rice or quinoa instead of couscous

**Suggested protein substitution: 220 gms (7.8 oz) of chicken breast
