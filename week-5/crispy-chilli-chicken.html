Crispy Chilli Chicken

Serves 2

613 kcalCalories
55.82 gProtein
4.23 gFat
83.12 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 150 gms (5.3 oz) of basmati rice
  • 1 broccoli, cut into smaller florets
  • 400 gms (14.1 oz) of chicken breast, diced
  • 15 gms (0.5 oz) of cornstarch
  • 1 red chilli, finely diced
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 5 gms (0.2 oz) of ginger, grated or finely chopped
  • 1/2 zucchini, diced
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of snow peas, trimmed and halved
  • 60 mls (2 fl oz) of soy sauce
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of honey
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of lime juice
  • 30 mls (1 fl oz) of light sweet chilli sauce

Method

  1. Cook rice according to packet instructions.
  2. Bring a small pot of water to the boil. Add broccoli and boil for 5 minutes, then drain and set aside.
  3. Toss chicken in cornstarch until fully coated.
  4. Heat a pan over medium-high heat and add chicken. Cook for 6-8 minutes or until browned.
  5. Add chilli, garlic and ginger and heat for 1 minute.
  6. Add broccoli, zucchini and snow peas and cook for 5 minutes.
  7. Combine soy sauce, honey, lime juice and sweet chilli sauce. Add to pan and stir to combine. Simmer until thickened.
  8. Divide rice into two serves and top with stir fry.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free soy sauce or tamari

**Suggested protein substitution: 340 gms (12 oz) of lean beef steak
