Chicken & Avo Sandwich

Serves 1

629 kcalCalories
55.16 gProtein
20.84 gFat
48.67 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 130 gms (4.6 oz) of chicken breast
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of reduced fat mayonnaise
  • 3 slices wholegrain bread
  • 1/4 avocado, mashed
  • 1/4 red onion, sliced
  • 1/2 tomato, sliced
  • 1/4 lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of baby spinach
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of reduced fat tasty cheese

Method

  1. Poach chicken. Once cooked, finely chop and combine with mayonnaise, salt and pepper.
  2. Assemble 1.5 sandwiches with mashed avocado, chicken mixture, onion, tomato, cucumber, spinach and cheese.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free bread

**Suggested protein substitution: 1 x 95g (3.4 oz) tin of tuna in olive oil
