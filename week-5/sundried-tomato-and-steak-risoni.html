Sundried Tomato & Steak Risoni

Serves 2

607 kcalCalories
40.81 gProtein
11.43 gFat
77.02 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of lean beef steak
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of cherry tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 red capsicum, finely diced
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of green beans, trimmed and halved
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of sundried tomatoes, drained and chopped
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of tomato paste
  • 150 gms (5.3 oz) of risoni pasta
  • 500 mls (16.9 fl oz) of chicken stock

Method

  1. Season steak with salt and pepper.
  2. In a large pan or pot, heat half the oil over medium-high heat. Add onions, garlic, cherry tomatoes, capsicum, green beans and sundried tomatoes and heat for 5-7 minutes or until vegetables soften.
  3. Add the tomato paste, risoni and stock and stir to combine. Turn the heat to medium-low and cover the pan with a lid. Allow to cook for 20 minutes or until risoni has cooked and liquid has been absorbed. Add more stock if required.
  4. Meanwhile, heat remaining oil in a separate pan over medium-high heat and cook steak to your liking. Allow to cool before slicing.
  5. Divide risoni into two serves and top with steak. Store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use rice or gluten free pasta instead of risoni.

**Suggested protein substitution: 240 gms (8.5 oz) of chicken breast
