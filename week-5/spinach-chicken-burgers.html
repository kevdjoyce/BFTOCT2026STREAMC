Spinach Chicken Burgers

Serves 2

591 kcalCalories
44.58 gProtein
20.55 gFat
51.34 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 250 gms (8.8 oz) of chicken mince
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of baby spinach, very finely chopped
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 5 gms (0.2 oz) of dijon mustard
  • 1/4 tsp of garlic powder
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 2 wholemeal rolls
  • 40 gms (1.4 oz) of reduced fat mayonnaise
  • 40 gms (1.4 oz) of reduced fat tasty cheese
  • 1/2 lebanese cucumber, sliced
  • 1 tomato, sliced

Salad

  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of baby spinach
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 1/4 red onion, diced
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of lemon juice

Method

  1. Combine chicken mince with spinach, breadcrumbs, egg, dijon mustard, garlic powder, salt and pepper. Form into two burger patties.
  2. Heat a non-stick frypan over medium-high heat and cook chicken patties for 5 minutes each side or until cooked through.
  3. Prepare the salad. Combine spinach, cucumber, tomato and red onion. Season with salt and pepper and drizzle with lemon juice and olive oil. Toss to combine.
  4. When ready to serve, assemble burger with mayonnaise, cheese, chicken patties, cucumber and tomato. Serve alongside salad.
  5. Store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free rolls

**Suggested protein substitution: 250 gms (8.8 oz) of turkey mince
