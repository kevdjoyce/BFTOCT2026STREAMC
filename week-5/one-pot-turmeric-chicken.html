One Pot Turmeric Chicken

Serves 4

612 kcalCalories
53.77 gProtein
7.17 gFat
78.64 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 800 gms (28.2 oz) of chicken breast, diced
  • 4 tsp of turmeric
  • 1 zucchini, diced
  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of mushrooms, sliced
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of lemon juice
  • 370 gms (13.1 oz) of basmati rice
  • 1 ltrs (33.8 fl oz) of chicken stock
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of baby spinach, roughly chopped
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of parsley, roughly chopped

Method

  1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add onion and garlic and sauté for 3-4 minutes or until soft.
  2. Add chicken and turmeric and brown the chicken for 4-5 minutes.
  3. Add zucchini, mushrooms and lemon juice to the pot and cook for another 5-8 minutes.
  4. Add the rice and stock and stir to combine.
  5. Cover and simmer on medium for 15-20 minutes, stirring every 5 minutes until rice is cooked and liquid is absorbed.
  6. Once cooked, stir through the spinach and parsley.
  7. Divide into four serves and store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 670 gms (23.6 oz) of lean beef steak
