Paprika Fish & Salad

Serves 1

612 kcalCalories
62.67 gProtein
33.2 gFat
11.05 gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of plain flour
  • 2 sprinkles of salt
  • 1 tsp of paprika
  • 250 gms (8.8 oz) of white fish fillet
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 5 gms (0.2 oz) of parsley, finely chopped
  • 15 mls (0.3 fl oz) of lemon juice
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of white wine vinegar
  • 20 mls (0.7 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/3 lebanese cucumber, diced
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of baby spinach
  • 1/4 avocado, diced
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of reduced fat tasty cheese, cut into cubes
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper

Method

  1. Combine flour, 1 sprinkle of salt and paprika. Add the fish and coat in the flour mixture.
  2. Combine garlic, parsley, 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of lemon juice, and vinegar.
  3. Heat 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of olive oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Cook fish for 3-4 minutes each side or until cooked through. Pour over the garlic and parsley sauce and allow to heat for another minute.
  4. Combine cucumber, tomato, spinach, avocado and cheese. Dress with remaining salt, pepper, lemon juice and olive oil.
  5. Serve fish alongside salad.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 250 gms (8.8 oz) of chicken breast
