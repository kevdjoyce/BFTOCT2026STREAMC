Chicken & Vegetable Biryani

Serves 2

618Calories
42.59gProtein
5.72gFat
91.99gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of basmati rice
  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 brown onion, diced
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of ginger, grated or finely chopped
  • 1/2 tsp of garam masala
  • 1/4 tsp of turmeric
  • 1/4 tsp of paprika
  • 1/4 tsp of cinnamon
  • 60 gms (2.1 oz) of high protein natural yoghurt
  • 100 gms (3.5 oz) of frozen peas
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 cauliflower, cut into smaller florets
  • 70 mls (2.4 fl oz) of chicken stock
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of lemon juice
  • 250 gms (8.8 oz) of chicken breast, diced

Method

  1. Cook rice according to packet instructions.
  2. Heat oil over medium heat. Add onion and cook for 5 minutes or until softened. Add some water if the pan looks dry.
  3. Stir in the garlic, ginger, garam masala, turmeric, paprika and cinnamon. Cook for 1 minute, stirring.
  4. Add the yoghurt and stir for another minute.
  5. Add peas, carrots, cauliflower and stock. Cook for 10 minutes or until vegetables are softened.
  6. Add the rice, salt and pepper. Stir for 3 minutes or until rice is evenly coated and hot.
  7. Remove from heat and drizzle with lemon juice.
  8. Panfry chicken in a nonstick pan for 7-8 minutes or until cooked through.
  9. Divide biryani into two serves and top with chicken. Store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 210 gms (7.4 oz) of lean beef steak
