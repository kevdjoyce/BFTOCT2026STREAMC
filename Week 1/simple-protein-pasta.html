Simple Protein Pasta

Serves 4

620Calories
44.84gProtein
10.04gFat
78.4gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 5 mls (0.2 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 cloves of garlic, crushed
  • 500 gms (17.6 oz) of lean beef mince
  • 1 zucchini, grated
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of mushrooms, finely diced
  • 500 gms (17.6 oz) of passata
  • 15 gms (0.5 oz) of rosemary, roughly chopped
  • 350 gms (12.3 oz) of pasta
  • 1 x 400g (14.1 oz) tin of cannellini beans, drained and rinsed

Method

  1. Heat oil in a large saucepan over medium-high heat. Add onion and garlic and sauté for 1-2 minutes.
  2. Add mince and cook, breaking apart, for 5 minutes or until browned.
  3. Add zucchini, carrot and mushrooms and heat for another 5 minutes, stirring to combine.
  4. Add the passata and rosemary. Turn the heat down to low and simmer for 30-60 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile, cook pasta according to packet instructions.
  6. Blitz the cannellini beans until a smooth puree is formed. Add to sauce and stir to combine. Heat for a further 10 minutes.
  7. Add pasta to the sauce and stir to combine. Divide into four serves and store in the fridge.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free pasta

**Suggested protein substitution: 500 gms (17.6 oz) of lean lamb, pork or chicken mince
