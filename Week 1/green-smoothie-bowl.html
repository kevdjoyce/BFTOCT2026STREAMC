Green Smoothie Bowl

Serves 1

620Calories
35.54gProtein
33.07gFat
42.12gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 1/4 avocado
  • 125 gms (4.4 oz) of frozen mango
  • 70 mls (2.4 fl oz) of reduced fat milk
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of baby spinach
  • 30 gms (1.1 oz) of whey protein powder
  • 1 banana, half sliced
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of shredded coconut
  • 20 gms (0.7 oz) of flaked almonds

Method

  1. Blitz all ingredients together except the sliced half banana and shredded coconut in a high speed blender.
  2. Mixture is designed to be thick however it is too thick, add a small amount of water until desired consistency is achieved.
  3. Serve in a bowl and top with sliced banana, shredded coconut and flaked almonds.
