Steak Power Bowl

Serves 2

597Calories
56.62gProtein
15.07gFat
53.03gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 150 gms (5.3 oz) of quinoa
  • 350 gms (12.3 oz) of lean beef steak
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 brown onion, sliced
  • 1/4 red onion, sliced
  • 1 1/4 red capsicum, sliced
  • 20 gms (0.4 oz) of baby spinach
  • 1/2 lebanese cucumber, diced

Method

  1. Cook quinoa according to packet instructions.
  2. Season steak with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat 5 mls of olive oil in a frypan over high heat. Cook steak to your liking. Remove from heat and allow to rest before slicing.
  4. Heat remaining oil over medium-high heat. Cook onions and capsicum for 5-10 minutes or until soft.
  5. To assemble, layer bowl with quinoa and top with steak, capsicum and onion, spinach and cucumber. Store leftovers separately in the fridge.

Notes

**Suggested protein substitution: 400 gms (14.1 oz) of chicken breast
