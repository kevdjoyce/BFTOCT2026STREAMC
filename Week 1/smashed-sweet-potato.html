Smashed Sweet Potato

Serves 2

598Calories
35.67gProtein
17.58gFat
66.03gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 800 gms (28.2 oz) of sweet potato, peeled
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 6 eggs, boiled to your liking and sliced in half
  • 1/2 avocado, mashed
  • 1/2 red onion, diced finely
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of lemon juice
  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of high protein natural yoghurt
  • 200 gms (7.1 oz) of cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 15 gms (0.5 oz) of coriander, roughly chopped

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 220 degrees Celsius (430 F) and line a tray with baking paper.
  2. Pierce sweet potato with a fork or knife in several spots. Microwave for 8 minutes or until softened.
  3. Transfer to lined baking tray. Cover with another piece of baking paper and use a rolling pin to flatten sweet potato until around 1 cm thick. Remove top piece of baking paper and sprinkle with salt and pepper.
  4. Roast smashed sweet potato in the oven for 20-25 minutes, or until the edges are crispy.
  5. Meanwhile, boil the eggs and combine mashed avocado with red onion and lemon juice.
  6. When ready to serve, place half smashed sweet potato on a plate and top with half the avocado, yoghurt, cherry tomatoes, boiled eggs and coriander. Store leftovers in the fridge.
