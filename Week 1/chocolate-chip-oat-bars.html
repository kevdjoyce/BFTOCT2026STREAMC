Chocolate Chip Oat Bars

Serves 12

169Calories
4.66gProtein
8.56gFat
17.51gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 160 gms (5.6 oz) of rolled oats
  • 140 gms (4.9 oz) of peanut butter
  • 60 mls (2 fl oz) of honey
  • 1 tsp of vanilla extract
  • 60 gms (2.1 oz) of dark chocolate chips

Method

  1. Line a baking tin with baking paper.
  2. In a medium bowl, mix together the oats, peanut butter, honey, vanilla extract and dark chocolate chips until fully combined.
  3. Transfer to baking tin and press the mixture down firmly to form an even layer.
  4. Refrigerate for at least 3 hours before cutting into 12 serves.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use quinoa flakes or brown rice flakes instead of rolled oats
