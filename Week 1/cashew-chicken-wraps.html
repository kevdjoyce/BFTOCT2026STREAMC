Cashew Chicken Wraps

Serves 2

610Calories
42.43gProtein
19.26gFat
60.59gCarbs

Nutrition per serve

Ingredients

  • 250 gms (8.8 oz) of chicken breast
  • 50 gms (1.8 oz) of snow peas, sliced thinly
  • 1/2 red capsicum, sliced thinly
  • 1 quarter head red cabbage, shredded or sliced thinly
  • 1 carrot, julienned or grated
  • 40 gms (1.4 oz) of cashews, roughly chopped
  • 40 gms (1.4 oz) of reduced fat mayonnaise
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of honey
  • 10 gms (0.4 oz) of dijon mustard
  • 10 mls (0.3 fl oz) of white wine vinegar
  • 1 sprinkle of salt
  • 1 sprinkle of pepper
  • 2 wholegrain wraps

Method

  1. Poach chicken and once cooked, shred.
  2. Combine shredded chicken with snow peas, capsicum, cabbage, carrot and cashews.
  3. Whisk together the mayonnaise, honey, dijon, vinegar, salt and pepper. Pour over salad and toss to combine.
  4. When ready to serve, place half the chicken salad in a wrap and roll up. Store leftovers in the fridge.

Notes

*GF adaptation: use gluten free wraps

**Suggested protein substitution: 210 gms (7.4 oz) of lean beef steak
